Notification Tester

Paste a raw log line (with or without the leading [timestamp] bracket - rules match against whatever ActGlobals gives as logLine, and it's not confirmed whether that includes the bracket or not) and fire it through the real rule set. If it matches, open notifications.html in another window to see the alert render - this runs the exact same matching code the live game path uses, not a simulation. Countdown timers are no longer authored here - those come from ACT's own native Spell Timers, viewable live in timers.html. Sample buttons below fire independently of this box - clicking one doesn't touch what's typed here, and typing here doesn't get affected by whichever sample you last clicked.
